What it is

A bar that knows you

Bar Ellington is a home bar that knows your bottles and learns your taste. It thinks with you while you mix, helping you make and name drinks that are yours alone. And behind every pour stands a community of makers who raise the craft together.

The Four Worlds

Choose where you drink

Bar Ellington is four bars in one. Pick a world and the whole experience follows you into it.

Gatsby

The Gilded Room

Step into the glow of the Jazz Age. A private room of gold, marble, and midnight blue, where every drink arrives like a celebration and every guest is someone worth remembering.

Interstellar

The Far Horizon

Beyond the last light, the bar goes on. Clean precision meets the warm amber glow of a distant giant, where discovery feels infinite and the next great drink is one you have not imagined yet.

Canvas

The Open Canvas

Every great drink begins as a blank canvas. Light, space, and craft, so the only thing that matters is what you create.

Tiki

The Endless Escape

Somewhere it is always warm. Rum, fire, and the sound of the tropics, an escape you can pour at home whenever the day asks for one.
